# Major-Project
Comparative analysis of the Pre-Windsor and Post-Windsor pharmaceutical regulatory framework in the United Kingdom, including preparation of a CTD Module 3 (Quality) dossier for Amorolfine Cream 5% w/v under Regulation 51B of the Human Medicines Regulations 2012.

## Overview

This repository contains the summary of my M.Pharm (Regulatory Affairs) major project completed at CHARUSAT University.

The project presents a comprehensive comparative analysis of the United Kingdom's pharmaceutical regulatory framework before and after the implementation of the Windsor Framework. It evaluates regulatory changes affecting MHRA marketing authorisation procedures, CTD requirements, pharmacovigilance, labelling and packaging, clinical trials, and UK-wide licensing following Brexit.

As the practical component of the project, a CTD Module 3 (Quality) dossier for Amorolfine Cream 5% w/v was prepared for a generic submission under Regulation 51B of the Human Medicines Regulations 2012 (as amended).

---

## Objectives

- Compare the Pre-Windsor and Post-Windsor UK pharmaceutical regulatory frameworks.
- Evaluate the impact of the Windsor Framework on MHRA regulatory procedures.
- Analyse changes in marketing authorisation pathways.
- Study CTD regional requirements for the United Kingdom.
- Prepare a CTD Module 3 (Quality) dossier for Amorolfine Cream 5% w/v.
